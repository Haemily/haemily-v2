# Future volunteering workflow

This workflow is intentionally documented but not implemented in the MVP prototype.

1. A member expresses volunteering interest.
2. The interest appears in an HSS moderator queue.
3. HSS contacts the member.
4. The member completes availability and approved-help preferences.
5. HSS activates or declines volunteer access.
6. Possible future statuses: Interested, Contact pending, Contacted, Setting up preferences, Active, Paused, Declined, or Withdrawn.

The current prototype only prepares an editable WhatsApp draft to a configurable HSS volunteer contact. It does not collect detailed availability, qualifications, addresses, exact locations, contact information, or preferred help categories during onboarding.
